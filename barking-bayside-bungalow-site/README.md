# Barking Bayside Bungalow Website

Free short-term rental website for Barking Bayside Bungalow.

## What to customize

### Photos
Replace images in:

public/photos

Use the same file names:
- hero.jpg
- living-room.jpg
- kitchen.jpg
- bedroom.jpg
- bathroom.jpg
- outdoor.jpg

### Calendar
In app/page.tsx, find the Google Calendar iframe and replace the src with your public Google Calendar embed link.

### Booking Form
In app/page.tsx, replace https://forms.google.com with your actual Google Form booking inquiry link.

### Contact Email
In app/page.tsx, replace "Replace with your email" with your email address.
